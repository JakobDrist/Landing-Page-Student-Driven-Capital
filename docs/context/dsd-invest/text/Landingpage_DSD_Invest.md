WEBSITE CREATIVE BRIEF  /  NOVEMBER 2026

DSD Invest

An investment institution. Built by students.

| DSD Invest should not look like a student society trying to appear professional. It should look like a serious investment organisation that happens to be built and operated by exceptional students. |
| --- |

The institutional idea

DSD Invest is envisioned as a national, student-driven investment organisation. It combines fundamental research, probabilistic forecasting, quantitative portfolio construction and formal accountability with an equally important mission: giving ambitious students the opportunity to perform meaningful professional work before graduation.

| Identity<br>An investment organisation that develops talent — not a talent-development club with an investment theme. | Design mandate<br>Communicate professionalism, selectivity, ambition and intellectual depth while remaining authentic to the organisation’s actual stage. |
| --- | --- |

The website has three jobs

01   Establish institutional credibility — Present DSD as a serious organisation with a coherent investment philosophy, clear governance and specialised functions.

02   Make membership desirable — Help ambitious students understand where they fit, what they will do, and why being selected matters.

03   Win partners and sponsors — Show organisations what their support makes possible, why it is valuable, and how to start a conversation.

THE HOMEPAGE NARRATIVE

Eight sections. One coherent story.

The homepage should introduce the institution quickly, then open clear paths to deeper content. The sequence below is a narrative guide, not a prescribed wireframe.

| 01 | Hero | DSD Invest; a decisive headline, one-line positioning and routes into the investment approach and membership. Suggested direction: “Student-driven. Institutionally minded.” |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 02 | Our Mission | Why DSD exists: exceptional students should not have to wait until graduation to begin doing consequential professional work. |
| 03 | Investment Approach | The central credibility section: fundamental company research → probabilistic forecasts → quantitative portfolio allocation → disciplined risk management and continuous updating. |
| 04 | Our Organisation | Founding Partners, Investment Committee, governance and the specialised teams; show how distinct functions contribute to one institutional process. |
| 05 | Founding Team | The people building DSD, their backgrounds, responsibilities and conviction. Trust through substance rather than elaborate self-promotion. |
| 06 | Careers & Membership | A compelling invitation to explore departments, member responsibilities, learning opportunities, standards and selective recruitment. |
| 07 | International Ambitions | International investment competitions, industry engagement and external validation as ambitions that support — not define — DSD’s identity. |
| 08 | Partnerships | “Invest in the next generation of investment talent.” Explain what sponsor support enables and offer a clear path to contact DSD. |

The narrative hierarchy

| Investment Approach<br>Builds credibility | Careers & Membership<br>Creates member demand | Partnerships<br>Converts sponsor interest |
| --- | --- | --- |

International competitions should reinforce DSD’s credibility through future external evaluation, not turn the organisation into a case-competition society.

THE MEMBERSHIP EXPERIENCE

Make membership feel earned — and worth pursuing.

The careers experience should borrow the clarity and seriousness of a professional financial institution, not the conventions of a typical student-club sign-up page.

Students should understand what DSD actually does, how they might contribute and what they stand to gain: responsibility, challenging work, development alongside talented peers, and a meaningful professional network. The primary message is not “join a finance club”; it is “begin building your career through real responsibilities.”

EXPLORE OUR DEPARTMENTS

| --- | --- |
| Macro & Markets | Economic conditions, market dynamics and macroeconomic scenarios.<br>Macroeconomics · Market research · Forecasting |
| Risk & Quant | Portfolio exposures, forecast uncertainty, model risk and quantitative analysis.<br>Statistics · Risk modelling · Quantitative finance |
| Portfolio Construction | Translate company and macro forecasts into allocations under risk constraints.<br>Optimisation · Portfolio theory · Decision models |
| Tech & Data | Build the software, data systems and analytical infrastructure DSD depends on.<br>Software engineering · Data pipelines · Fintech |

What each department should reveal

A distinct and explorable experience covering the team’s purpose, example projects, responsibilities, expectations, preferred competencies and connections to the wider investment process.

Prestige must be credible

•  Selective, transparent entry.  A rigorous process based on ability, ambition, reliability, curiosity and potential — not artificial scarcity.

•  Real contribution.  Substantive tasks, high standards, accountable responsibilities and opportunities to develop or advance.

•  Accessible ambition.  Do not imply applicants already need years of professional experience; select for ability to learn as well as existing skills.

THE PARTNERSHIP EXPERIENCE

Support exceptional talent. Enable real responsibility.

The partnerships experience should explain the institutional opportunity, what external support can enable and why a sponsor might choose to become involved.

FOUR PARTNERSHIP AREAS

•  Research & Technology.  Professional financial data, software, research tools and technical infrastructure.

•  Education & Mentorship.  Training, workshops, advisers and access to experienced professionals.

•  International Programme.  Competitions, conferences, international engagement and travel.

•  Institutional Development.  Recruitment, governance, organisational capabilities and long-term sustainability.

The sponsor journey deserves a dedicated partnership page, not just a contact form at the end of the homepage. It should make benefits and possible forms of collaboration understandable, offer an unmistakable route to contact DSD and, as evidence develops, display partners and measurable annual outcomes.

| --- | --- |

Credibility is part of the design

The website must distinguish adopted policy from future ambition. Show departments as operational only when they are; describe international participation as an ambition until secured; and do not imply management of external capital or regulatory authorisations that are not in place.
